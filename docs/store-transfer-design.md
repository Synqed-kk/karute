# Customer ↔ Store Transfer — Design (店舗間移動の設計)

**Status: DESIGN — build timing per §5.** Authored 2026-06-11 (3-lens council +
judge) at Liam's direction after 浜野's transfer to 銀座 surfaced the question.
Companion to docs/versatility-master-plan.md and the multi-store spec.

## 0. Liam's laws (non-negotiable)

1. **Staff moving stores does NOT move their customers.** A staff transfer is
   one update to profiles.store_id — zero customer writes. 浜野's 24 代官山
   customers transfer ONLY if the same person appears in 銀座's own customer
   records, name-matched at onboarding with dry-run + sign-off (P4 discipline).
2. **Customers may stay when their staff moves** (Liam, explicit): many will
   remain at 代官山 with a NEW 担当. Staying is the DEFAULT — moving is the
   exception that must be proven. The stay-behind handover flow (§2b) exists
   for exactly this.
3. One source of truth (synqed-core): nothing ever moves; only labels change.
   Duplicates are MERGED (both sides may carry real data), never deleted blind.
4. The first-timer nightmare (customer visits the other store, treated as a
   stranger) must be impossible BY CONSTRUCTION, not by policy.

## 1. The decided data model

DECIDED — option (c): one nullable home_location_id on synqed-core customers as a mutable operational LABEL (reporting, marketing attribution, default store), plus an always-DERIVED visiting-store set from immutable per-store visit FACTS (location_id stamped at creation on appointments, karute_records, recordings — and on pack_redemptions/ticket_packs for money provenance). 'Stores this customer visits' is never stored; it is `select distinct location_id` over the UNION of the fact tables, so a customer belongs to 銀座 the moment a 銀座 visit fact exists, by construction, with zero human-maintained membership table to drift. Identity, search, karute history, customer_memory, ticket_packs/redemptions, and lifecycle are BUSINESS-level reads with no store predicate ever — this kills the first-timer nightmare at the data layer. Store labels never partition identity: a per-store customer list is a VIEW (home=S OR visit-fact-at-S); customer search is always business-wide; profiles.store_id scopes staff default views only, never customer lookup. Transfers move the label, never data: 'transfer to 銀座' = one UPDATE of home_location_id + one immutable ledger row; zero rows move in appointments/karute/memory/packs. Karute keeps what it owns (stores table, profiles.store_id for staff); NO karute-side customer↔store sidecar — that would fork identity across two databases. AMENDMENTS TO ANTHONY'S SPEC (/Users/liam/karute/docs/multi-store-synqed-core-spec.md — must be agreed BEFORE core implementation, the spec as written says 'when karute passes a location_id, scope to it' with no carve-out): (1) split location_id semantics by entity — on appointments/recordings/karute_records it is an immutable per-store FACT stamped from the karute_active_store cookie; on customers rename it home_location_id, a mutable LABEL changed only via a dedicated transfer operation; (2) the optional location_id filter applies ONLY to list/count/dashboard endpoints and NEVER to get-by-id, search, or history reads — and business-wide SEARCH must be written into the spec explicitly, not assumed; (3) define per-location counts: store cards count by home label ('customers who belong here'); activity-at-store counts (derived from visit facts) are a separate, separately-named metric; (4) backfill all 374 existing customers + their appointments to 代官山 (the is_primary store), explicitly NOT null — null means 'unassigned' and a null backfill makes the first Ginza dashboard meaningless; backfill runs BEFORE Ginza import, scoped to location_id IS NULL with a cutover timestamp (idempotent, cannot re-sweep); (5) a dedicated updateCustomerHomeLocation mutation (customerId, toStoreId, actor, reason — NO business_id parameter) distinct from generic customer PATCH, so karute can RBAC-gate and audit it; (6) stamp location_id facts on pack_redemptions (where redeemed) and ticket_packs (where purchased) from day one so per-store P&L and future inter-store settlement are computable. business_id stays the tenant + coaching boundary unchanged; coaching never keys on location_id; this slots into the versatility master plan as-is and generalizes to every multi-location tenant.

## 2. Transfer UX

- SINGLE TRANSFER ENTRY POINT — a deliberate kebab action 「店舗を移動」 on the customer profile identity card, plus a read-only 所属店舗 row showing home store + derived visited stores. NEVER a field inside CustomerEditDialog/CustomerForm — an org-level move must not sit one mis-tap from a phone-number edit. Gated by a new customers.transfer capability (added to CAPABILITIES in src/lib/auth/permissions.ts, seeded into owner+manager presets only, per-staff toggleable), enforced server-side with requireCapability from day one — do NOT copy stores.ts's interim requireOwnerBusiness pattern.
- CONFIRM DIALOG — shows current home store; destination picker with NO preselection (confirm disabled until chosen); explicit from→to line (代官山 → 銀座); a 'what changes' list (ホーム店舗 = store list membership, report attribution, default booking location) and a 'what never changes' list with the contract in plain language: 「履歴・カルテ・回数券は全店舗で見えたままです。変わるのはレポート集計と所属表示だけです」; honest reversibility note (non-destructive metadata; undo = transferring back). Server validates both stores belong to the caller's business_id; destination comes ONLY from explicit selection, never the active-store cookie (explicitly 'not a security boundary' per stores.ts).
- AUDIT LEDGER — every transfer writes one immutable row to a NEW karute-side customer_transfers table (id, business_id, customer_id, from_store_id, to_store_id, actor_staff_id, reason, source 'manual'|'bulk_onboarding', batch_id nullable, created_at). Required because the 設定 AuditLogSection is a paywalled empty stub with zero write infrastructure (verified). Surfaced at 設定 > 店舗 > [store] > 「顧客の移動履歴」 (filterable table, batch drill-in, gated by existing audit.view) — free-tier operational safety, NOT folded into the paywalled section. Customer profile shows its last transfer as a one-line provenance note (2026/06/11 代官山→銀座・山田店長). Revert = one-click inverse transfer through the same action, exactly as safe as the original.
- BULK — there is deliberately NO 'move all of staff X's customers' button, ever; it contradicts Liam's directive (1) and is the zero-mistake footgun. Bulk re-homes happen only through the store-onboarding import wizard (Ginza flow) with dry-run + owner sign-off, source='bulk_onboarding' + shared batch_id per row.
- CROSS-STORE VISIT — lists are store-scoped, SEARCH IS BUSINESS-WIDE: front-desk search at 銀座 always searches the whole tenant; out-of-store hits render in a quiet 「他店舗のお客様」 group, never hidden. On the profile, when active store ≠ home store, a neutral informational chip 「代官山のお客様」 next to the name — not a warning; full karute history, packs, booking, recording all behave normally; the new session stamps location_id = 銀座 while home stays 代官山.
- RE-HOMING IS MANUAL-WITH-NUDGE, never automatic — after 3 consecutive visits at a non-home store, staff holding customers.transfer see a dismissible suggestion (「直近3回は銀座でのご来店です。ホーム店舗を銀座に移動しますか？」) opening the standard confirm dialog; dismissal remembered until the pattern resets. Auto re-home rejected: silent data movement is the fragile-mistake class being designed away, and it misfires for genuinely two-store customers.
- WRONG-LABEL PREVENTION AT CREATE — visible active-store indicator on the customer-create surface so staff working under the wrong active-store cookie catch the wrong home label at write time; harm is bounded (label only) and the audited transfer makes correction cheap.

### 2b. Stay-behind handover (Liam's addition, 2026-06-11)

When a staff member transfers stores, their home-store customers who do NOT
move need a new 担当. This is a 担当 reassignment, never a store transfer:

- After a staff store-change, the customer list filtered to that staff shows a
  dismissible banner for managers: 「浜野は銀座に移動しました。代官山に残る
  N名の担当を再割り当てしますか？」 → opens the staff-filtered list where each
  customer's 担当 is changed individually via the existing profile edit dialog
  (deliberate per-customer choice — relationships are not bulk-assignable).
- NO automatic reassignment, NO bulk "give them all to X" (the same footgun
  class as bulk store transfer). An assisted flow may PREFILL a suggestion
  (e.g. the staff who served them most in visit facts) but every confirm is
  per-customer.
- 担当 (assigned_staff_id) and home store are INDEPENDENT axes: a 銀座-homed
  customer may keep a 代官山 指名 if that's the human truth (they book around
  their stylist's schedule). The system never forces them to agree.

1. STEP 0 — 浜野's own move is exactly one UPDATE to profiles.store_id (代官山→銀座). Zero customer writes. Her 24 代官山 customers keep home=代官山. Customer store attribution must never derive via assigned_staff_id→profiles.store_id.
2. STEP 0.5 — pre-import gates: synqed-core backfill (374 customers + appointments → 代官山) completed and reconciled BEFORE the Ginza import (ordering is load-bearing — a later backfill would sweep Ginza-created customers into 代官山); Ginza QR sync stays OFF until per-location sync_config + the QR identity fixes land.
3. STEP 1 IMPORT — collect 銀座's own customer records (sheet/CSV) into the dump-customers.mjs shape (/Users/liam/karute/scripts/import/). Strongly encourage phone numbers in the Ginza records — they are the tie-breaker that prevents wrong-person merges.
4. STEP 2 MATCH — dryrun.py (READ-ONLY): exact NFKC-normalized 1:1 name match of Ginza records against the ENTIRE business-wide customer book, never per-store and never just 浜野's 24 — a Ginza-recorded customer assigned to other 代官山 staff still satisfies Liam's rule. Cross-store exact-name hits do NOT auto-claim without a second factor (phone/kana/DOB); a near-miss section (kana-normalized, edit-distance ≤1, phone match) catches 浜野's hand-typed typos and is human-adjudicated only; ambiguity is reported, never guessed; claimed-map prevents two sheet rows hitting one customer. Report groups: matched-assigned-to-浜野 (her 24-candidates) vs matched-other-staff vs unmatched.
5. STEP 3 DRY-RUN REPORT + SIGN-OFF — matched pairs shown with name, phone/DOB tie-breakers, last 代官山 visit, pack balance; unmatched Ginza records → will be created as new 銀座 customers; unmatched 代官山 customers explicitly listed as untouched. STOP. Owner/Kitano sign-off button restating the counts (P4 procedure law — same 289/289 discipline). Default for any unconfirmed ambiguous row = CREATE a separate Ginza customer: a wrong split is mergeable later, a wrong merge is not.
6. STEP 4 EXECUTE (idempotent, execute.mjs pattern) — matched person = the SAME customer row (no duplicate identity): gains Ginza import/visit facts, home transfer to 銀座 only as per the signed-off plan, one customer_transfers row each (source='bulk_onboarding', shared batch_id); unmatched = new customer with home=銀座 and location_id stamped explicitly at creation; re-runs skip already-transferred rows via the ledger as idempotency key. This is the ONLY way 浜野's customers transfer — same person in Ginza's own records, surviving dry-run sign-off.
7. STEP 5 RECONCILE — Σ per-store home-label counts + unassigned = business-wide total (the P4 reconciliation discipline); spot-check that a transferred customer's packs/history/memory are fully visible from 銀座; verify 代官山 dashboards unchanged for untouched customers.

## 4. Guards — top risks made impossible by construction

- FIRST-TIMER NIGHTMARE → impossible by construction: get-by-id, search, karute history, memory, packs, lifecycle are business-level reads with no store predicate, ever; the location_id filter is contractually defined (spec amendment 2) to apply to list/count/dashboard endpoints only; business-wide SEARCH is written into Anthony's spec explicitly. A 代官山 veteran walking into 銀座 is found with full history, packs and memory intact.
- STRANDED PACKS/MONEY (~¥8.7M-class liability) → transfer is BY CONSTRUCTION a single UPDATE of the home label on the same synqed customer_id; the API offers no insert/copy path; every karute-side table (ticket_packs, pack_redemptions, customer_lifecycle, memory, contacts, dismissals) keys on customer_id so a transfer needs ZERO karute writes; any 'copy customer to store' affordance is banned.
- WRONG-PERSON MERGE (同姓同名, the one unrecoverable corruption) → cross-store exact-name hits never auto-claim without a second factor (phone/kana/DOB); ambiguous and near-miss rows route to mandatory human confirm with tie-breaker columns; the default on no-confirm is create-separate (reversible) over merge (irreversible); the sign-off gate is non-optional for every future location.
- CROSS-TENANT CONTAMINATION (the only path transfer could touch coaching) → updateCustomerHomeLocation takes NO business_id; the action resolves the caller's business server-side and hard-verifies toStore.business_id === customer.business_id before writing — cross-business transfer is unrepresentable at the API shape; coaching never keys on location_id (already spec law); regression test alongside the spec's 'location_id never widens scope past businessId' rule.
- RACE / DOUBLE-COUNT → transfer runs under pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext(business_id)) (the pattern already at src/actions/stores.ts:194); alert queues stay computed business-wide in one pass (alerts-core.ts) and are then PARTITIONED by home label — a partition of one list cannot double-count or drop a customer mid-transfer; business-wide money totals (回収リスク, 未消化総額) render only on the owner's all-stores view.
- WRONG ACTOR / FAT-FINGER → dedicated customers.transfer capability (owner+manager presets, per-staff toggleable), explicit confirm dialog only — never an inline dropdown or edit-form field; destination store never read from the active-store cookie; requireCapability enforced server-side from day one.
- IRREVERSIBILITY → immutable customer_transfers ledger (who/when/from→to/reason/source/batch); undo = applying the inverse event through the same single-column action; one-click revert on the history row; the ledger doubles as bulk idempotency key and the first real feed for the audit-log section.
- 浜野-DERIVED RE-BUCKETING → customer store attribution comes ONLY from customers.home_location_id; profiles.store_id drives staff lists exclusively; add a regression test asserting a staff store change alters zero customer-facing store filters or counts.
- QR SYNC IDENTITY CORRUPTION (open TODAY, pre-Ginza) → fix the name-based find-or-create against a single 200-row page (route.ts:131-138 — duplicate window already open at 374 customers) with name-filtered lookup or full pagination NOW; make sync_config per-location (provider+location_id unique) before Ginza's QR account exists; add qr_customer_links (qr_customer_id ↔ synqed customer_id) via a one-time dry-run/sign-off link pass so binding is by stable id, never name; stop overwriting visit_count/is_existing_customer downward (MAX-merge or karute-derived) so Ginza's QR cache can never flip a 24-visit veteran to 'new'.
- MISSING VISIT FACTS → derive the visiting set from the UNION of appointments + karute_records + recordings (a walk-in handled without an appointment still leaves a fact); a missing active-store cookie at write time is an ERROR, not a silent null.
- TRUST EROSION DURING TRANSITION → no store-filtered UI ships before the synqed-core filter is live (the spec's own no-regression stance); per-store views flip only after Σ per-store counts + unassigned reconciles to the business total.

## 7. Migrating pack-holder economics (Liam's 残3-4 scenario, 2026-06-11)

Liam's scenario: customer buys a 10回券 at 代官山 (cash taken there), moves to
銀座 with 残3-4, burns the remainder at 銀座, re-subscribes at 銀座. "Keep that

### 7.1 Attribution model

§7.1 ATTRIBUTION MODEL — three ledgers, never blended. Liam's scenario decomposes into (a) CASH/SALES ledger = ticket_packs.location_id (where purchased, immutable): pack revenue (total_price ?? size×unit_price, matching withUsage()) recognized at purchased_at, attributed to the purchase store forever — 代官山 keeps the full credit for the 10回券; the 銀座 re-subscribe is 100% 銀座 revenue; transfers NEVER restate sales (closed-period integrity — under franchise the 代官山 franchisee literally banked the cash). (b) DELIVERY ledger = pack_redemptions.location_id (where redeemed, immutable): every row where redemption store ≠ pack's purchase store IS the settlement event, valued at the pack's unit_price as neutral notional. (c) RELATIONSHIP ledger = customers.home_location_id (mutable label): drives alert-queue ownership and workload views, never money. UNCONSUMED LIABILITY ships as TWO NAMED LENSES, never one merged number [PER-STORE-DASH]: 未消化(販売ベース) = Σ remaining×unit_price over active packs by PURCHASE store — the unearned-revenue truth (代官山 keeps carrying her 残3-4 yen after she re-homes; correct and desirable — it drains to zero as 銀座 serves her, leaving the audit trail Kitano's formula prices); 未消化(担当ベース) = same Σ by HOME label — whose staff chase the customer. BRIDGE (computed live, no journal entries, no stored inter-store AR/AP): 他店持ち出し(S) = remaining value of S-purchased packs homed elsewhere; 他店持ち込み(S) = remaining value of packs homed at S but purchased elsewhere; rendered as one reconciliation line per store money block. 回収リスク stays by-HOME by construction (atRiskValue in src/lib/packs/alerts-core.ts sums over the home-partitioned contact queue) — never build a by-purchase 回収リスク; chasing the customer is the home store's job regardless of who holds the cash. DELIVERY metrics [AT-GINZA]: 他店券消化(S) = redemptions at S against packs purchased elsewhere (count + ¥ at unit_price); 自店券の他店消化(S) = own packs drawn down elsewhere; 役務提供高(S) = Σ unit_price over ALL redemptions at S ('production', mirroring the §1-amendment-3 home-vs-activity split); all three labeled 参考値・精算ではありません. RECONCILIATION IDENTITY (every view must satisfy): byHome(S) = byPurchase(S) − 持ち出し(S) + 持ち込み(S); Σ_S 持ち出し = Σ_S 持ち込み; Σ_S byPurchase + unstamped = Σ_S byHome + home-unassigned = the business-wide unconsumedTotal from alerts-core — the business number is the referee. Today's dashboard money block (business-wide unconsumedTotal/atRiskValue) needs ZERO changes [NOW = nothing].
